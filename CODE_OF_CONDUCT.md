# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This is a project about a disease people live with every day; assume
the person you are replying to is tired.

## Specifically

**Technical disagreement is welcome and wanted.** Saying the design is wrong,
the cryptography is unsound, or the whole idea is a bad one is a contribution.
Saying it rudely is not necessary to say it clearly.

**Do not give medical advice**, and do not treat anything here as such. People
sharing loop data are managing a condition where being wrong has consequences.

**Do not post other people's data**, and be careful with your own — a glucose
trace is close to a fingerprint. Keys, invites and endpoint ids identify a
device; scrub them from logs before pasting.

**Assume the maintainer is one person.** There is no team, no on-call and no
SLA. Security reports are read promptly; feature requests may sit.

## Not tolerated

Harassment, personal attacks, or making someone's diagnosis, body, treatment
choices or use of a DIY system into a joke. DIY looping attracts strong opinions
from people who do not have to live with either the disease or the decision;
this is not the place for them.

## Enforcement

Reports go to the maintainer through
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/alex-poor/diaswarm/security/advisories/new)
or a direct message on GitHub. There is no committee. Responses range from
"please stop" to a block, and the maintainer will say which and why.
