See the glucose of someone who has chosen to share it with you. Ayni is a viewer — and only that.

* **No insulin, no pump, no closed-loop code.** If you're looking for an artificial pancreas, this isn't one.
* **Private by design.** Readings travel directly phone-to-phone, sealed before they leave the sender — no account, no server, no one in the middle who can read them. Sharing ends the instant the sender revokes your key.
* **One scan to pair** — the person sharing scans your code and hands back their own address in the same motion.
* **Every reading shows its age.** A follower's real danger isn't an error on screen; it's a stale number that looks current.

Ayni is Quechua for *reciprocity*: your phone carries other people's sealed records so that theirs carry yours when your phone is away — and no phone can read what it holds for anyone else.

To share your own data you build the AndroidAPS add-on yourself, from the project's repository; it is deliberately not distributed.
