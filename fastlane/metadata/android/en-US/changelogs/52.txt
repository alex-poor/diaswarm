The graph is now the one the loop app draws: the trace over every raw reading
kept faintly beneath it, a target band, and delivered insulin in a panel of its
own. Boluses, carbs and basal appear for the first time — they were always being
sent, and were being discarded on arrival.

Readings are coloured against a range this phone sets, because the person you
follow publishes a target rather than a range. It is labelled on screen, and can
be turned off.

Requires a sharing phone updated on or after 11 September 2026. An older one is
reported as unreachable.
