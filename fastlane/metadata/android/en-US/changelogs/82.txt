The app no longer gets killed after an hour.

A bug in the networking library queued the same failed connection attempt over
and over, and the app grew until Android reclaimed it — usually within the hour,
and worst on a phone moving between networks. A fix from that library's issue
thread is carried here. Six hours of watching now pass without it.

The person you follow can give themselves a name, and you see the name instead
of sixteen characters of hex. They choose it when they make the invite; if they
have not, nothing changes.

Ayni now shows what it is carrying for other people — the swarm only works
because each phone holds a little of everyone else's, and you could not
previously see that happening.
